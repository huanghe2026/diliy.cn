The five pillars

Geoyuan is organised around five roles. Each is both an open position and a pillar of how we build. The Psychology Expert designs how we screen and assess new members — including the test you just took.

Note: our Psychology Expert owns recruitment screening & fit assessment — the science behind the challenge on /test/.

PR
Programmer
builds / maintains

Owns the site, our internal tooling, and the infrastructure that keeps the network state standing. Ships features and automates the boring parts.

We're looking for: Full-stack or systems mindset. You’ve deployed something real and can read a stack trace at 3 AM.
Apply for this role
AR
Artist
visuals / brand

Shapes how Geoyuan looks and feels — design system, illustrations, motion. Makes the matrix beautiful, not just functional.

We're looking for: A portfolio that shows taste. Comfortable with both static design and living, animated interfaces.
Apply for this role
AI
AI Expert
models / agents

Runs our intelligent systems — the Q&A, content generation, and anything agentic. Turns models into things that actually help the crew.

We're looking for: You’ve shipped with prompts, RAG, or agents. You know when a model helps and when it just talks.
Apply for this role
DA
Data Analyst
metrics / insight

Watches the recruitment funnel and community health. Turns noise into decisions — who to reach, what to build next.

We're looking for: You think in funnels and cohorts. SQL + a charting tool is enough; curiosity is required.
Apply for this role
PS
Psychology Expert
screening / fit

Designs how we assess and welcome new members — including the challenge on /test/. Reads motivation and fit better than any resume.

We're looking for: Background in assessment, behavior, or research. You can tell signal from noise in people.
Apply for this role
