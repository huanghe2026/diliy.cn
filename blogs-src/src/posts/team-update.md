A short status post. We don't usually talk numbers, but building in the open
means occasionally telling people what we're actually doing.

## Where we are

- **12 active members** on the crew, spread across reverse, web, pwn, and
  infra. Up from 4 at the start of the year.
- **47 CTF wins** logged across the season — mostly small, a couple we're
  genuinely proud of.
- **∞ coffee** consumed. The metric is non-negotiable.
- **3 0-days** found and responsibly(ish) reported. None of them yours.

## What we shipped

- This blog. It only took us nine months to stand up a markdown folder.
- A rewritten infra playbook so `ghost` stops getting paged at 4 AM.
  Partially successful.
- Internal tooling for rapid binary triage — `v0idk1tten` is leading it and
  it's already faster than our old `file && checksec && strings` ritual.

## What's next

We're doubling down on the "network state" thesis from the main site. That
means more public writing, more reproducible builds, and less mystery. The
goal isn't to be loud — it's to leave a trail someone else could follow.

If you're reading this and you think you'd fit, the door is the same as
always: prove yourself, leave a geoyuan card that says `KAI MEN`, and
we'll find you.
